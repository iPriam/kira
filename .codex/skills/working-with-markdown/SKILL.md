# Markdown

Follow `caveman:caveman` in `full` mode.

## Headings

Use plain noun phrases.

Reject slogans, aphorisms, jokes, and headings written as sentences. Use `Decoders`, `Layers`, or `The gates`, not `Fence the unsafe`.

## Style

Delete filler, hedging, pleasantries, and sentences justifying preceding rules.

Use no em dashes or prose arrows. Use punctuation or separate sentences.

## Order

Lead with the conclusion. Follow with evidence or instructions.

Delete throat-clearing, previews, callbacks, and document narration.

## Deletion

Delete paragraphs that do not change the reader's next move.

Prefer facts over framing. Delete lines repeating headings or neighboring sections.

Let length reflect importance. Split large topics. Do not pad small ones.

## Form

Use tables for short enumerable facts. Use lists when order or membership matters. Use prose otherwise.

## Pruning

Repair weak claims instead of hedging.

After editing, delete duplicated or overlapping sections.
